The Sovereign Guarantee: A Framework for Post-Labor Governance and the Transformation of Public Security in the Age of Artificial General Intelligence

The Automation Singularity: Analyzing the Impending Structural Collapse of Traditional Labor Markets

The global economic architecture is currently navigating a period of transformation characterized by a velocity and scale that exceeds the precedents established during the nineteenth-century Industrial Revolution. As artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning transition from speculative innovations to the primary engines of productivity, the traditional social contract—predicated on the exchange of human labor for economic subsistence—is entering a phase of terminal obsolescence. Recent projections from the World Economic Forum indicate that information processing and AI systems will drive approximately 86% of global labor market transformation by the year 2030. This is not merely an era of incremental efficiency but a structural "cognitive industrial revolution" that threatens to decouple economic growth from human employment.

In the United States, the saturation of AI across the private sector is already nearing a critical threshold. Evidence suggests that over 70% of companies currently use or plan to integrate AI to augment or replace human activities within their workforce. While early narratives focused on "augmentation"—the enhancement of human capability through digital tools—the empirical data provided by the McKinsey Global Institute (MGI) reveals a more disruptive trajectory. According to current assessments, technically demonstrated technologies could theoretically automate approximately 57% of all U.S. work hours. When this theoretical potential is mapped against the current job market, it is revealed that roles with the highest potential for automation account for roughly 40% of the total jobs in the United States.

The implications of this shift are profound. The traditional reliance on high-skill education as a safeguard against technological unemployment is being undermined by the rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) and multimodal systems such as Claude 3.5, Gemini 2.0 Flash, and OpenAI’s model o1, which have demonstrated the ability to outperform humans in competition-level mathematics and Ph.D.-level scientific reasoning. As these "agentic" systems gain the capacity for multistep problem-solving and nuanced analysis, the "technical automation potential" moves from routine manual labor into the core of professional knowledge work, including legal services, administrative management, and clinical diagnostics.

The concentration of this pressure is expected to manifest first in the "agent-centric" and "robot-centric" roles that comprise the backbone of modern service and manufacturing economies. As the "technical automation potential" is realized, approximately 11% of the global workforce is predicted to be unlikely to find a path toward upskilling or redeployment, creating a permanent class of vulnerable citizens. This vulnerability gap necessitates a radical reimagining of the state's role, shifting from a regulator of labor to the primary guarantor of citizenship livelihood.

The Mandate for State Responsibility: Redefining Livelihood in the Post-Work Era

The systemic capture of the workplace by machine intelligence necessitates a fundamental transfer of responsibility for citizen survival from the individual to the government. In a post-labor society, the government must adopt the "Sovereign Guarantee," a policy framework where the state assumes the total responsibility for the livelihood of all citizens. This shift is predicated on the understanding that the massive wealth created by AI—estimated to contribute up to $19.9 trillion to the global economy by 2030—is a collective dividend of human history and must be distributed as such.

The Fiscal Transition: From Labor Taxation to Non-Labor Rents

The current fiscal model, which relies heavily on the taxation of human wages, is unsustainable in an economy where human labor is no longer the primary driver of value. As AI replaces human workers, the traditional tax base will erode, requiring a transition toward the taxation of "non-labor rents". This includes windfall taxes on the technology corporations that own the AI infrastructure, as well as levies on the returns of highly productive autonomous systems themselves.

This fiscal rebalancing provides the resources necessary for the state to provide "Guaranteed Livelihood Allowances" and "Universal Basic Services" (UBS). Proponents of Fully Automated Luxury Communism (FALC) argue that we are entering a period of "unthinkable plenty," where technological progress can eliminate the scarcity upon which capitalism depends. In this paradigm, the government’s role is to ensure that "luxury pervades everything," moving beyond the logic of "waged work" to a future characterized by abundance and universal access to the essentials of flourishing.

The synthesis of these models—a "Sovereign Guarantee" that provides both a cash floor and comprehensive basic services—is increasingly viewed as the only viable response to the "useless class" phenomenon described by Yuval Noah Harari.

Reimagining Public Security: The Transformation of Policing into a Livelihood Protection Force

The most radical requirement of the "Guaranteed Livelihood" framework is the total reconfiguration of the police. In a society where the government assumes full responsibility for the survival of the citizenry, the traditional role of the police must evolve into a "Social Security Police" or a "Livelihood Protection Service".

The Philosophical Crisis: Human Meaning and the "Useless Class"

The "useless class" refers to individuals who are economically and politically irrelevant from the viewpoint of the machine-dominated system. The government’s responsibility for livelihood must therefore include the provision of "meaning" and "purpose".

Conclusion: Toward a Post-Labor Social Contract

The impending capture of the workplace by artificial intelligence and autonomous machines is an irreversible technological fact. Governments must accept full responsibility for citizenship livelihood. The "Sovereign Guarantee" is the mechanism by which we move from a world of "struggle against irrelevance" to one of "shared opulence and meaningful freedom".
